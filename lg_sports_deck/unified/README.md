# LG Sports Deck — unified fictional integration prototype

**INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT ONLY — not the completed online test site, a client installer, or a production system.** Use invented `.invalid` accounts and test passwords only. Do not enter real customer information, real passwords, payment keys, camera credentials or client data. Do not expose this build to the public or ask customers to use it.

The user chose a later restricted private live test once safeguards are ready; the present build is not ready for that test. This document is for internal development and reproducing isolated tests, not a request for the user to perform acceptance testing. No purchase or paid service is needed or has been authorized here.

## What this milestone connects

- A booking-first English interface and one isolated SQLite database connect fictional player accounts, individual recording-notice acknowledgements, Open Play and court-rental simulated payments, booked participants, queue/check-in, matches, rankings and sample sales. The supplied LG Pickleball logo is unchanged. The displayed court photo is an illustrative stock image by [Frank Schrader / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/empty-pickleball-court-with-net-in-summer-sun-29820786/), not the venue.
- Fictional group members must each have an enabled player account and independently acknowledge the test notice. This is **not legal consent** or an approved real companion-consent flow. The checkout amount is a simulation only. Open Play blocks overlapping rentals on both courts; rentals use whole hours. Transaction tests exercise a final slot, a court conflict and session/rental concurrency. These are not real payment or distributed-system guarantees.
- A full test session offers free waitlisting. Marking an unchecked-in fictional player as a no-show retains the simulated sale and creates **unsent** notices. As a provisional test rule, while enabled waitlist members exist, reopened places can be taken only by those members; the first successful simulated checkout gets the place. This placeholder rule is not the final payment, expiry, late-payment or reconciliation policy.
- The fictional portal can issue an actual QR image for a test session and decode it locally via browser camera or a selected test image. The one-current-per-session challenge lasts three minutes; rotating it invalidates the prior one, only its hash is stored, and signed-in fictional players must have simulated paid registrations. Duplicate scans are idempotent and the check-in plus FIFO queue entry are transactional. The older static test-token input remains as a clearly labeled legacy fallback; it must not be used for real operations. A QR can be photographed/shared while valid and does not prove physical presence. Session check-in windows, device/anti-abuse behavior and real venue use remain unapproved and untested.
- Players return to the provisional queue on match end even with a missing result. Pending scores do not affect rankings; player representatives from both teams must confirm. Scores and rankings apply only to Open Play. Staff with `queue.manage` can move waiting entries up or down; the action checks the entire observed order and classifications so a concurrent check-in, match, result update or reorder requires a refresh rather than silently overwriting it. New check-ins append to the end. The current suggestion still prioritizes groups of four winners or losers where possible; manual team selection overrides it. Final grouping, pending classification and partner persistence remain provisional.
- A rental match is created from four accounts included in its simulated rental. Participating players can start/stop a fictional sample recording. The unified portal uses a looping, visibly labelled stock pickleball video—not a camera feed and **not footage of LG Sports Deck or its players**—to produce a separate clip per match. Tests check same-court rollover, the other court's isolation, replay access for participants and Superuser only, and refusal of access to Admin, Manager and outsiders. For this fixture only, all four rental match participants get sample-replay alerts and unsent mail jobs; the real rental recipient policy remains undecided.
- The unified prototype records durable **test-only notification jobs** atomically for fictional booking confirmations, waitlist openings, Open Play queue assignments, replay readiness and confirmed Open Play results, plus sample rental replay jobs. The existing booking confirmation targets the test booker; a separately configured fictional venue inbox adds its own unsent job for a new simulated-paid booking. The real player/companion recipient policy for group bookings is not finalized. Unique event keys, a manual local simulator, bounded retry/backoff for known simulated failures, lease-expiry/ambiguous-outcome handling and an idempotent receipt table are exercised. An optional, separate loopback-only TLS sink can also consume fictional test messages; its acceptance is not real delivery. Unknown outcomes are held for review rather than blindly retried. These simulated states do not prove real email was accepted or delivered; no worker runs automatically and no Gmail account is connected.
- A MIME renderer can only produce unsent messages for `.invalid` recipients/senders and an HTTPS `.invalid` test origin. Assignment/replay/result test links lead to a fictional match screen that still requires sign-in and server-side authorization; the link itself grants no video access. Automatic **real-media** expiry/deletion remains absent. The older primary mail schema idempotently backfills some isolated test events; new in-site alerts, extra queue/rental mail and venue-booking jobs have **no historical backfill**. None of these is a production data migration.
- Signed-in fictional players have a persistent website notification center and unread badge for Open Play **assignment**, **sample replay ready**, and **confirmed ranking update**. A sample replay alert may appear even when the score is missing; a ranking alert appears only after both teams confirm. The page polls every 15 seconds while signed in and visible, has mark-read/open actions, shows unread first and displays up to 100 updates at a time; older unread items become reachable as others are read. No browser push is implemented. New alerts and extra mail jobs are transactional, player-owned and fictional-only. They do not send real email.
- A Superuser can create fictional Admin or Manager accounts with **no permissions by default** and grant/revoke individual test actions. The server checks authentication, Origin, CSRF and action permissions; hiding a button is not an access control. Only Superuser assigns grants. There is no Admin/Manager replay grant. Revocation is checked on subsequent server requests; in-flight behavior is not guaranteed. When the UI learns of a permission change, it clears stale test views; already observed data cannot be remotely erased.
- A Superuser-only **Payment & email** test screen can change/clear a fictional `.invalid` sender, change/clear a separate fictional venue booking-notification recipient, and generate/replace an explicitly non-GCash fictional payment QR. Server-side checks deny players and Admin/Manager even if all current grants are assigned; revision checking prevents stale saves, and test audit records changes without addresses or QR payloads. The image is **not** the check-in QR and cannot accept money. The venue setting affects only future unsent test jobs; none of these placeholders alters checkout, connects Gmail, sends email or accepts real QR images. Real provider, venue-recipient and sender management still need implementation and security review.
- Daily/monthly sample totals are broken down by service and simulated payment method. They are not a settlement, official receipt or BIR invoice. Rates and limits are configurable in test data; paddle rental is a displayed rate only, not a transaction workflow.

The fictional mail simulator and loopback-only SMTP lab use a fixed allowlist of `primary` and `extra` buckets. `primary` contains prior booker, waitlist and Open Play replay/result jobs; `extra` contains only assignment and rental sample-replay jobs. Each uses a separate receipt table; numeric IDs cannot be mixed across buckets. Only the manual test lab can consume these jobs, and a fake sink's acknowledgement is not live delivery.

The older `../staging/` and `../integrated/` prototypes remain separate, have switchable fictional identities and are **not** secured by this application. Unified composition removes the older account-only checkout/operations routes and does not mount those demos. Do not reuse their database as a migration or live database.

## Isolated payment and email labs — no live configuration

These are development modules, **not the finished integrated online test**. The user supplied a temporary payment QR and a venue recipient for confirmed/paid booking notifications, then expressly chose the same Gmail as the outbound sender. The conversation connector reports that Gmail as the primary/default account, but this does **not** connect the website as a sender. Neither real input is stored in the fictional portal, code or static assets. Superuser needs to be able to change the payment setup and venue recipient later; future sender controls are separate. Keep fictional and future live settings separate and replaceable. Do not request or paste real passwords, GCash MPIN/OTP, Gmail App Passwords, payment API secrets or camera credentials into chat.

`integration_settings_test.py` backs the internal Superuser-only page with additive fictional settings, individually authorized GET/change actions, CSRF/Origin checks, no-store responses, optimistic revision and minimal audit events. It rejects real email addresses, uploaded images and credential fields. The QR is generated only from an invented `LGSD-PAYMENT-TEST-ONLY` payload; the raw **fictional** token is stored to regenerate the image and never represents a payment destination. Current portal checkout ignores this QR; email jobs ignore the sender setting. The separate fictional venue recipient creates one unsent venue job atomically on each new simulated-paid booking in addition to the player job; it never backfills prior bookings. Changing or clearing it affects future jobs only, and existing jobs retain their captured test recipient. The current page previews the latest 50 such jobs for Superuser; this is a **test-only policy**, not a decision about pending real messages. Before building the real version, determine whether the selected provider uses merchant-linked checkout or dynamic per-transaction QR rather than static uploads, independently verify any destination and email ownership, reauthorize a replacement sender, add step-up/MFA, secret management, monitoring and audit, and keep in-flight transactions bound to their original provider configuration. A static QR by itself cannot verify a payment.

- `manual_qr_lab.py` is a **fictional-only manual review** experiment, unmounted and OFF by default. The optional integrated test mode accepts only invented `.invalid` users and `FAKE-QR-...` references; it disables the ordinary instant-simulation HTTP checkout. A player creates a pending Open Play or rental quote and submits a fake reference claim; neither a claim nor a screenshot is proof, and no pending request holds a place or creates a booking/job. The test Superuser or Admin/Manager individually granted `payments.manual_verify` must enter an independently checked matching fake reference, observed amount and explicit attestation. Rechecks, fake reference uniqueness, idempotence and transactional booking/unsent jobs prevent the tested duplicate and oversell cases. Fake amount mismatches or paid-but-unbookable fake intents remain visible as exceptions, without a refund/resolution action. The test UI shows only a fake reference suffix and accepts no screenshot; the full fake reference is not stored. A claimed reference can be corrected before review, but the server matches the latest claim. The lists show up to 100 items and are not a production backlog solution. The mode cannot display or inspect the real QR, access a bank/GCash account, verify or receive money, deliver email, support real users, or guarantee first-real-payment order. Real-reference security, recipient verification, transaction ownership, fees, late payments, real dispute handling and audited exception resolution remain unimplemented.
- `payment_lab.py` is an **offline fake-provider** exercise using a separate disposable, sentinel-checked SQLite gateway. It makes a server-priced fictional intent with an idempotency key, verifies an invented HMAC callback, and fetches the fake provider snapshot instead of trusting browser returns. Atomic booking and unsent notification creation occur only after a matching fake success. Duplicate callbacks, concurrent final-place/court claims, outages, missed events, price changes, amount/currency/reference mismatches, failed-then-paid and reversals are exercised; a paid but unbookable fake transaction is retained for manual review, not silently discarded or automatically refunded. Its snapshot versions, HMAC format and event names are invented; they are **not** a GCash or any real provider integration. The pending test intent holds no capacity, and the first *processed fake success* wins—not necessarily the earliest real-world payment. A real guarantee needs provider-specific ordering, capacity, late-payment and exception decisions. Nothing here sends a real charge, refund, QR, webhook or network request.
- The existing portal still uses its legacy **one-click simulated checkout**, not the new payment lab. Neither the fake-gateway controls nor its callback are mounted as HTTP routes. A real provider and fee/exception policies remain unselected; no account or subscription is created.
- `smtp_lab.py` can manually deliver only `.invalid` MIME messages to a **127.0.0.1-only fake SMTP sink** after pinned-certificate STARTTLS and invented test authentication. Tests use a short-lived certificate generated by local OpenSSL. Fake acceptance, temporary/permanent rejections, lost response after DATA, expired leases and disabling a user during submission are exercised. Ambiguous submissions are not blindly retried. The fake sink never relays mail; a `simulated` receipt is not Gmail acceptance or inbox delivery. A cross-lab test connects a fake paid booking to an unsent job and then to the fake TLS sink.
- `gmail_test_sender.py` is a separate, **not mounted and not automatically invoked** Gmail SMTP test-message adapter. If a future operator privately supplies a real Google sender credential and an explicit recipient allowlist, and separately calls `send_one_test(..., explicit_send=True)` with authorization, it can send one fixed, clearly labeled **real test email** via `smtp.gmail.com:587` using verified STARTTLS. It is intentionally disconnected from fictional accounts, notification jobs and all web routes; it has no stored address, QR, password or default sender, and no live Gmail connection has been attempted. Current tests use a mocked SMTP connection or forcibly redirect to a loopback sink with reserved example addresses; neither contacts Google. SMTP acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery. This is not a production sender or a substitute for account/recipient verification, secret management, real outbox integration, limits, monitoring and review.

## Internal developer setup — isolated computer only

These commands require a terminal on the developer's own machine. They are **not client installation instructions**. Use a completely new disposable directory and never point the bootstrap or app at an existing venue/client database. The bootstrap creates only a fictional Superuser; there is no HTTP bootstrap endpoint and no default credential. The optional `--session-label` from the older account demo must not be used here: create test sessions through the unified operations UI so that a unified schedule is recorded.

From the workspace root:

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv
. .venv/bin/activate
python -m pip install -r lg_sports_deck/unified/requirements-test.txt
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD"

# This directory must be new and used exclusively for fictional data.
mkdir -p lg_sports_deck/unified/data/media
export LG_UNIFIED_TEST_DB="$PWD/lg_sports_deck/unified/data/unified-test.sqlite3"
export LG_UNIFIED_TEST_MEDIA="$PWD/lg_sports_deck/unified/data/media"
python -m lg_sports_deck.accounts.bootstrap \
  --db "$LG_UNIFIED_TEST_DB" --email owner@example.invalid
# Privately enter and repeat an invented TEST password when prompted.

export LG_UNIFIED_TEST_ORIGINS="http://127.0.0.1:8016"
export LG_UNIFIED_INSECURE_LOOPBACK=1
# Optional: the bundled labelled stock match sample only; no real cameras.
export LG_UNIFIED_SYNTHETIC_FFMPEG="$(python -c 'import imageio_ffmpeg; print(imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe())')"
python -m uvicorn lg_sports_deck.unified.devserver:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8016
```

Visit `http://127.0.0.1:8016` on that same computer only. The example deliberately permits **insecure cookies for loopback-only testing**; do not bind it to a network or use real data. Database, media and allowed Origin have to be explicitly configured. If testing under isolated HTTPS, omit `LG_UNIFIED_INSECURE_LOOPBACK`, configure the exact HTTPS Origin and separately arrange TLS; that still does not make this build safe for deployment. The bootstrap refuses to create a second Superuser and does not reset an existing database. Development dependencies are pinned for tests, not a reviewed production lockfile.

The fictional Access page lists `payments.manual_verify`; it is default-deny and only Superuser can grant/revoke it. It cannot authorize real verification. Normally the lab remains unmounted. For **internal developer QA only**, use a separate disposable test database/media folder and set `LG_UNIFIED_FICTIONAL_MANUAL_QR=1` before starting the unified test server. This exposes a clearly marked fake-only player request and staff review UI, not a real payment screen. It does not accept a QR image, real reference, real customer or money. Existing instant simulation routes return 409 in this mode. If the disposable database contains pending or exception-state fake requests, starting without the opt-in fails closed instead of hiding them; re-enable the fictional mode to inspect them. No migration or real-data use is supported.

For this test UI, `queue.manage` adds assignment controls but `session.view` and `queue.view` are also needed to select and inspect a queue. `score.enter` needs `queue.view` to list matches; attendance actions need `session.view` to select a session. Only Superuser or staff granted `attendance.qr.display` can issue or rotate a fictional session QR; staff also need `session.view` to choose the session in the UI. A signed-in fictional player with a simulated paid registration can scan it. The browser camera requires a supported secure context and permission; the image-file fallback reads the file locally. QR images and camera frames are not saved or uploaded by the scanner; only the decoded challenge is sent to the test server. These grants remain separate; replay permission is intentionally absent. The app is designed for **one process/worker** in the synthetic test, not multi-worker CCTV operation.

The offline browser decoder is `jsQR` 1.4.0, MIT-licensed, vendored as `static/vendor/jsQR-1.4.0.js` with `jsQR-LICENSE.txt`. Its vendored file SHA-256 is `bc40c8a15196236b2314db0856f72ca0b49980cd5413b8c852a7349f5fee0859`; the npm package tarball used for extraction had SHA-256 `b5299b37917a1fe7a8cab9dd5cc6b8accf82663add80abe5bf7761a921cc6602`. QR generation uses pinned `qrcode` and Pillow versions listed in the internal test requirements. These dependencies have not had an independent production security review.

## Optional sample-only deletion experiment

This is **disabled by default** and must never be used with real CCTV, customer footage, an existing media directory or backups. To exercise it, set `LG_UNIFIED_TEST_SAMPLE_RETENTION=1` *only for a new empty non-symlink directory and disposable fictional database*, alongside the FFmpeg setting above. A marker binds that folder to that DB. Only clips created and finalized after opting in are enrolled; older files are never backfilled. Each new sample gets an exact **ready-at + seven days** test deadline in the same transaction as readiness. Replay access is denied at expiry even if the worker is stopped; while the single-process test app is running, an opt-in loop checks due samples approximately once a minute. It denies linked/outside paths, retries failed deletion, and exposes a minimal test status. Restart triggers another sweep; downtime can delay actual file removal. It cannot delete previously downloaded copies, any backups, NVR, cloud or real camera media and is not a durable or production retention service. Do not point it at a client folder or reuse a real database.

Separate internal desktop/mobile browser QA covered the opt-in fake manual flow: player request/claim, default-deny staff, Superuser grant, reference mismatch, correct fake confirmation, rental amount exception and staff revocation while signed in. These tests are evidence of prototype behavior only, not approval for real users or a security audit.

The user selected the venue Gmail as sender. The Gmail account is connected to this conversation, but **not** to the web app; no actual mail has been sent. ## Critical gaps before an online test or launch

- **Money and booking:** the user selected a private test using the existing QR with **manual account-side checking**, not automated provider confirmation. A real pending-payment/review workflow, authorized staff access, secure verified reference/amount/recipient checks, duplicate prevention, non-overselling, exception/reconciliation, disclosures and real confirmation must be built and reviewed before a real charge. A screenshot alone is not verification. Manual review cannot guarantee the original first-successful-real-payment rule or immediate booking. For later automated confirmation, a GCash-capable provider and secure verified/idempotent webhooks/reconciliation would still be needed. Neither fake lab implements a live workflow. The daily Open Play start times are not yet supplied.
- **Accounts/security:** real address ownership verification, recovery, MFA, safe staff onboarding, production-grade anti-automation and distributed throttling, TLS/proxy/host configuration, audit controls, privacy, dependency review, monitoring, backup/restore and independent security testing are absent or incomplete. The fictional acknowledgement is not a legal consent system. The internal matching of known player names is not an approved privacy-preserving companion discovery flow.
- **Email, QR and timing:** the web app has no real email delivery or connected Gmail account; the separate explicitly gated Gmail test sender has only been exercised against mocks/local fake TLS, not Google or any real inbox. The QR image, decoder and three-minute challenge work only in the isolated fictional test; they are not a deployed, approved or physical-venue-tested check-in system. A copied QR remains usable within its lifetime, the old static token fallback is still present, and camera/device, abuse, offline fallback and remote-sharing risks require design and review. Live check-in and match/rental time windows are **not enforced**. The waitlist and notices remain test fixtures. Determine the approved no-show, queue, matching, partner-change, correction/dispute and exception policies before implementation.
- **Cameras/media:** no CCTV/RTSP or NVR is connected. The sample recorder is single-process and lacks production-safe ownership, crash recovery, durable operations, secure distributed storage and monitoring. Actual hardware, network, footage quality and same-court rollover need on-site verification. The user chose **seven days after video-ready**, but only an opt-in, sample-only best-effort cleanup experiment exists. Automatic real-media deletion including backups/NVR/cloud and downloaded copies is not implemented. Real rental recipients remain open. Review Philippine privacy obligations before any real capture.
- **Other operations:** paddle transaction/inventory flow, production queue controls and final partner/team-rating rules, BIR-compliant invoicing if required, report distribution, accessibility and deployment are not complete. Sample digital confirmation is not a tax invoice. Provider, equipment, storage, hosting and network costs remain undecided; obtain current quotations and approval before purchasing.

Never interpret a passing test, screenshot or simulated checkout as a real booking, payment, email, camera recording or production-readiness certification.
